Questions people usually ask

Do I need to be technical to use this?

No. We set the agent up for you. You link your email with a couple of clicks, then read a short daily overview. If anything is unclear, we will walk you through it.

Which email accounts can I link?

Gmail and Outlook are supported in this service. You can link more than one account, including a general inbox and a bookings or accounts address.

Will the agent send emails for me?

Not on its own. It can prepare a draft for you to review. Nothing leaves your inbox until you are happy with it.

What does “looked after for you” mean?

We set up and look after the agent that works inside your dashboard. You do not have to configure tools or keep anything up to date — that is part of the service.

Can I try it before I commit?

Yes. Use the form below and we will get in touch to arrange a walk-through, then a short trial on your own inbox if it feels like a good fit.
